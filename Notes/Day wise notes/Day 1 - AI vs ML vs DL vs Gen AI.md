# AI vs ML vs DL vs Gen AI
resource link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qYNweeDHiyU

- AI:
    - Artificial Intelligence is trying to simulate with a computer that would match or exceed human intelligence.
    - Intelligence is abilility to learn, infer and reason.
    - AI started as a research project in 1950's

- ML:
    - Machine Learning is a subset of AI, where machine learns from data and discovers patterns.
    - It is good at prediction, spotting outliers.
    - ML became popular in 2010's

- DL:
    - Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning. It uses Neural Netrworks.
    - Neural Networks are ways that we simulate in a computer how brains work. 
    - It is called Deep, because there are multiple layers in Neural Network.
    - We are not actually able to fully understand why we get the results we do, because there are so many layers in the Neural Network. 
    - These became popular in 2010's

- Gen AI:
    - The most recent advancement in AI 
    - Foundational Model - LLM. They use type of Neural Networks
    - These are called Generative as they generate new text, para etc. 
    - There are Audio, Video models as well.
